#+title: What facematch is
#+language: en

* What facematch is

A package that ranks candidate photos by how probable it is that they show the
person of one or more master photos.

You choose the photos in rimu, Tuatara's directory list, with its preview
showing each one. You mark the master photos, which show the person you look
for, and the candidates, which may or may not. facematch compares them and
lists the candidates with the most probable first. Each has its probability
and the words the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI) uses
for it, such as "Almost certain" or "Unlikely".

It is a package, not part of the editor, and it does no face recognition
itself. The comparison is done by the facematch engine, a project of its own
at github.com/YardQuit/facematch. The engine is InsightFace with the
AuraFace-v1 model and the YuNet face detector, made into one AppImage. The
package starts it in the background, shut in Tuatara's sandbox (chapter 4).

** Loading it and the engine

The package never downloads the engine. What runs is the file that
=*facematch-program*= names, which you fetched and checked yourself. The
package's README gives the steps to fetch the engine, check it and load the
package. This manual starts after that.

To see whether the engine is found, run =M-x check-health=. It lists "Faces
ranked by probability" with the engine's path, or says how to get the engine.

** What the engine needs

The engine's own README says how to build it instead of downloading it. On
Arch and Omarchy, an AppImage needs FUSE 2 to run, from the package =fuse2=.
