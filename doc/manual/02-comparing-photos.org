#+title: Comparing photos
#+language: en

* Comparing photos

Mark the photos in rimu, compare them, and read the ranking.

Photos are chosen in rimu, so everything here happens in a rimu list. Open one
with =C-x d=. The preview shows the photo under the cursor.

** Marking the masters and the candidates

1. Open the directory with the master photos in rimu. The master photos show
   the person you look for.
2. Press =g m= on a photo to mark it as a master. This is Tuatara's own
   command, =rimu-mark-master=. An =M= appears in the first column. Mark one
   photo, or several of the same person. Several are averaged into one
   template. Press =u= to take a mark away.
3. Open the directory of the candidates. It may be another directory. Press
   =m= on each candidate, as for any rimu operation. A candidate has =>= in
   the first column.

Masters can be marked in any rimu list, because they are often kept in a
directory of their own. Candidates are the photos marked in the directory the
list shows. Marks kept in other directories are not taken.

** The ranked list

=C-c f f= (=facematch-compare=) compares every master photo marked, in any
rimu list, with the candidates marked in this directory. The engine runs in the
background, so you can go on working. When it has finished, a rimu list named
=*facematch*= opens. With =C-u C-c f f=, the command first asks for a detection
confidence for this run, which starts at 0.8, the strict one. Without it, the
setting =*facematch-detection-confidence*= is used (chapter 4).

The list shows the masters first. A line apart come the candidates, the most
probable first, each with its ranking beside its name. Moving through the list,
the preview shows each photo. Marks and rimu's other keys work as in any list.

#+begin_example
  ~/pictures/:
  3 candidates ranked against 2 master photos (the least alike two 0.6123 alike, 99.0% one person): AuraFace-v1 ...
  master                                       masters/aroha-rotorua-1.jpg
  master                                       masters/aroha-rotorua-2.jpg

   99.0%  Almost certain     similarity 0.5312                        photos/p17.jpg
   62.4%  Likely             similarity 0.2474  likeliest of 3 faces  photos/p03.jpg
    1.2%  Almost no chance   similarity 0.0081                        photos/p11.jpg
  not compared: no face found                  photos/p20.jpg
#+end_example

The line over the list says how many candidates were ranked against how many
master photos, which model and detector found and compared the faces, and what
the probabilities were calibrated on. With several masters it also says how
alike the two least alike masters are, and how probable it is that they show
one person. When that is under 50%, the line adds that the masters may not all
be the same person.

Each candidate's line gives:

- the probability that it shows the master's person, in per cent. It is shown
  in the theme's info face from 80%, its warning face from 45% and its error
  face below that;
- the ODNI's words for that probability, from the table below;
- the similarity behind the probability, a number from the engine;
- =likeliest of N faces= when the photo has more than one face. The face of the
  candidate that is likeliest is the one ranked;
- a photo's name last.

A photo that could not be compared, for instance because no face was found in
it, is listed after the others, with =not compared= and the engine's reason.

#+table: commands facematch-compare

** The words

The words follow the ODNI's scale of estimative probability, from its
Intelligence Community Directive (ICD) 203.

| Words            | Probability |
|------------------+-------------|
| Almost certain   | 95-99%      |
| Very likely      | 80-95%      |
| Likely           | 55-80%      |
| Even chance      | 45-55%      |
| Unlikely         | 20-45%      |
| Very unlikely    | 5-20%       |
| Almost no chance | 1-5%        |

** Ranking in the list you are in

=C-c f r= (=facematch-rank-here=) compares the same way, but shows the ranking
in the rimu list you are in. Beside each photo's name come its probability,
words and similarity, with the preview on the right:

#+begin_example
99.0%  Almost certain  similarity 0.4447  p17.jpg
#+end_example

The lines of the files that were not compared are taken out, as =K= takes lines
out. The files themselves are not touched. The candidates' marks are taken
away, and the masters' =M= is kept. Press =C-c f r= again to show the list as
it was.

The ranked list =*facematch*= belongs to =C-c f f= alone. A new comparison
closes an earlier =*facematch*= first, so that its ranking is never shown
beside photos of another comparison.

** When nothing happens

The commands say why they did not start:

- not in a rimu list: candidates are marked in a rimu list and compared from
  it;
- no master photo is marked: =g m= marks one;
- no candidate is marked in this directory: =m= marks one;
- the engine is not found: the message says how to get it.

When the engine ran but compared nothing, the message gives its reasons, one
per photo. Only files are taken. A directory marked as a master or a candidate
is left out.
