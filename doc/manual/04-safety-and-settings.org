#+language: en

* Safety and settings

How the engine is kept from your files, and the settings that change a run.

The photos may be evidence, and a picture can be made to harm the program that
reads it. So the engine runs shut in Tuatara's sandbox:

- it sees private copies of the photos under names of its own, such as
  =master-1.jpg= and =candidate-2.png=. It never sees the photos' directories
  or names;
- its AppImage is unpacked once, outside the sandbox because it is your own
  program, into Tuatara's cache. It is run from there read only, so no run can
  change it for the next one;
- it has no network, and nothing of yours but its own files;
- it runs in the background, with at most 16 GiB of memory, and is stopped
  after =*facematch-seconds*=.

The copies are deleted when the engine is done. The photos are only read.

** The settings

Set them in the package's =:config= in your init file:

#+begin_src lisp
(load-package "facematch"
              :source "https://github.com/Tuatara-Editor/tuatara-facematch"
              :config
              (setf *facematch-seconds* "30m"))
#+end_src

#+table: settings *facematch-program* *facematch-detection-confidence* *facematch-seconds*

A value of =*facematch-detection-confidence*= outside the range stops the
comparison with an error. =C-u C-c f f= sets one run's value without changing
the setting.
